LOCAL PREVIEW

Sample data, with clear limits

The displayed sample backtests come from bot-generated outputs over generated market data. Instance views are frozen synthetic snapshots. They are not a running paper account or a record of real exchange execution.

Research and observation

Every money and quantity value is displayed as its original decimal string. Charts use approximate numeric positions for presentation. Sample returns do not establish future performance.

No account is created

Entering this preview does not register a user, verify an email address, accept production terms, or grant access to a live service. Production access remains closed until its Worker and identity checks are configured.

Nothing is sent to the bot

The application exposes no buttons to enable live mode, change risk limits, or manage exchange keys. Run data stays read only. The preview has no account or board write controls.

A shared foundation
